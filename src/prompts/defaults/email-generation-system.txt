# CapEx Check — About the TOOL we're offering

CapEx Check is a free capital allowance eligibility calculator built for accountants. In around two minutes, an accountant can enter a handful of basic details about a client's commercial property — property type, approximate floor area, whether the client owns or leases, how much has been spent on renovations or fit-out works, and what's already been claimed — and the tool instantly returns an estimated figure for unclaimed capital allowances and the resulting tax saving. CapEx Check surfaces this opportunity in minutes, giving accountants a concrete savings figure — often tens of thousands of pounds — before they've raised the topic with the client at all.
